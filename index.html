Problem 1

Not putting in the right efforts or simply the lack of efforts. Actions and words are both necessary and I am missing either of those.

A change

Words

I want to be more vocal about my feelings and thoughts. I never even complimented you, even though I had these thoughts in my mind. Whenever I think you look so beautiful, or elegant, these thoughts are somehow forever trapped in my mind.

I should express them in the future and be more expressive with my words. It will change a lot. I think I began trying that during our conversation last night. I will actively change this.

A change

Actions

While action can certainly include buying gifts and flowers, the small gestures can be more important. I don’t have experience knowing what might be the right gestures, but I believe these are some I should try.

In addition to practical help when you need it, I want to offer small acts of kindness: giving you small things, or sending thoughtful texts along the day (less bro-like) to cheer up your mood. As you mentioned yesterday, you like hugs, which I also like. I will hug you a lot if I ever notice you might be feeling down, or off for the day.

Problem 2

Failing to understand you from your perspective, being insensitive to your emotions.

A change

Your perspective

At least for the initial stage, for every action or event, I should consciously put myself in your shoes. This will feel unnatural, and I will definitely need your assistance until I can adopt it as a habit, without needing to constantly remind myself to do it.

I believe I am capable of doing it, but it will take immense effort, and a period where I need to constantly remind myself to always think and sympathize from the other person’s perspective in everything I do. I will also notice more when other people do this, especially learning from people like James.

Problem 3

Not being emotional present while only being physically present. Sometimes, I even manage my schedule poorly I am not physically present.

A change

Listening

I will actively listen to what you have to say, and affirm it first before providing actual feedback, or perhaps no feedback at all. Making our interaction a comfortable space for deep conversation is essential, and that requires more active listening, perhaps even without interruption.

In the past, I have assumed the role of giving feedback anytime someone came to talk to me about something, so that is something I need to fix for sure. Furthermore, I need to be more honest with my thoughts. I need to be more open to sharing my feelings instead of suppressing them. This can make our interaction more emotionally intimate than an academic conversation.

A change

Time

This is more of a practical change, considering that we are both very busy and have our own schedules. I think it is important we talk about the time we are willing to give going forward, and how we can accommodate such a schedule. Especially, I need to adjust my horrible schedule.

One solution we proposed is waking up earlier in the morning to spend time together prior to classes, where meeting up in person can help decide the plan for the day without needing to make the plan on text, and where we are not only seeing each other at night. In addition, it will brighten my day to see you in the morning and help me feel more prepared for the day, and I hope you do too.

I hope you will
